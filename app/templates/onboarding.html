One rule covers most of it: everything a node needs comes from a node above it, in its own path. And every path has to finish with an ending.

Input sourcehow players get into the journey
  • doesputs the player into the journey, and tells everything below it which currency the player is in
  • ruleone per journey
  • watcha journey has to start with an input source, always, with no exceptions

A journey is nodes in a line, and each one reads what the one above it left.

Two screens on the ones you will actually place.

Promotionone offer, made to the player
  • doesoffers the bonus to the player and waits. When they accept it, the rest of the path runs
  • watchthe terms of the bonus are set here, so this is the object to open when a player argues about them
Multipurpose Promotionoffers more than one promotion at once
  • doesshows the player several promotions so they can look at them and pick one. Whichever they pick, they carry on down that flow
  • watchit gives the player nothing by itself. Every option under it needs its own promotion

Two nodes that look alike and are not.

A plain Promotion is one offer. A Multipurpose Promotion is several, and the player picks.

Depositwaits for the player to pay in
  • doesholds the player until they deposit the minimum, then lets the path carry on. It gives nothing itself
  • watchit has to come after a promotion. Anywhere else and it has nothing to attach the deposit to
Casino FreeSpingives the free spins
  • doeshands the player their free spins, and carries on once they have played them
  • watchthe game and the value of each spin are set on this node
Casino Bonusthe wagering on what they won
  • doestakes what the spins won and holds it as bonus money. The player has to bet it through a set number of times, the wagering, before it becomes real money they can take out
  • watchwithout it the winnings are paid straight out with no wagering at all, which is almost never the offer we agreed
Waitholds the player for a while
  • doesstops the player here until the wait time is up, then carries on with the next node
  • watchthe clock starts when the player arrives at it, not when the journey starts

The chain that actually pays: a condition, then the spins, then the wagering.

Each one reads the promotion above it.

Decision splitsorts players by a rule you set
  • doeschecks each player against your rule and sends them down the matching path. Everyone who matches nothing goes down the last one
  • watchthat last path counts as a path, so it needs an ending like every other one
On-site messaging, email, SMStells the player something
  • doessends the content you wrote to the player
  • watchafter a message lands, the next node is a wait, a split or an ending, never another send. Only a failed send goes straight to the next channel — that is the fallback

Splits choose a path. Message nodes only talk.

Neither of them gives a player anything.
