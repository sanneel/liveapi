How it works

This clones the captured {{ brand_name }} tournament comms journey and swaps in the copy from your pasted sheet: the Notification (contract 1), the Pop-up (Cat-fish, contract 5), the SMS (behind its required {{ sms_prefix }} prefix) and the Email — EN & ES, each field written by name so one language never lands in the other's slot.

Every channel is pointed at your link's path. The old Smartico deeplink (#_smartico_dp=dp:gf_tournaments&id=…) is gone — it only ever addressed the captured tournament.

The script CREATES the draft and then SAVES it (POST then PUT). The save is what finalises the canvas; skipping it was what left nodes unconnected.

If any required field is missing from the spec — including the tournament window — the script stops with a clear error and nothing is created.
